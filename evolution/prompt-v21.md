# Prompt V21 - Parallel-Lane Memory-Bank Harness

Migrate the private `apitools` harness from its original single, unpadded `M`
milestone series to the current lane-aware memory-bank contract. Preserve
completed history while allowing future product domains to register milestones
under distinct uppercase letters and expose independent work to the unattended
runner.

Use permanent zero-padded IDs, parser-compatible status tables, an explicit
lane map, cross-lane priority and dependency rules, and unnumbered candidate
directions. Do not rewrite historical evolution snapshots, reclassify completed
milestones merely for tidiness, require an optional goal protocol, or change
the public `apitools` product boundary.
